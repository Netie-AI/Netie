# Offer ladder (direct English)

Say the work in this order. Owner 26 Aug 2026: start with a world-class landing page and marketing, then name agent / OCR / database as the stack on top. We are a strong IT shop that already builds this. First job is the best way to reach a customer who already landed on their site.

## 1. Landing page and marketing

We build a better landing page from their real copy and photos, plus the marketing around it, so a visitor who lands on the site knows the offer and can become a customer. This is a new selling page, not a rewrite of a Coming Soon menu. RM 500 unlocks the first draft. After they see it, the rest of the page work is quoted in writing. Usual full landing band is RM 1,000 to RM 5,000. Honor any lower price already named in that thread.

Build the page with `.cursor/skills/scroll-craft-landing/SKILL.md` and `.cursor/skills/mengto-frontend/SKILL.md` when it is a real landing, not a Coming Soon fill-in.

## 2. Marketing agent, OCR, and database

A marketing agent that tracks campaigns, leads, and what was sent. OCR on documents. A system-level database (DMS-class) that can grow with them. Public proof is the invoice workbench and ops board on https://netie.ai/hire/ not a private Jumpwin file. This sits on top of the landing page. It does not replace it.

## 3. Agentic operations

Agents that work in that database: tracking marketing, follow-ups, answers on the page so a visitor can get a real answer before they ask a useless question, and daily ops. Quoted after written scope. If it is one internal workflow, RM 20,000 operations checkout can follow that scope. Do not invent a new Stripe product.

## First mail

Name step 1 in plain words. One sentence that they are getting a landing page and marketing, not a Coming Soon rewrite. RM 500 for the first draft. One sentence that agents / OCR / database exist as a later build. No Stripe link in a cold first mail.

## If they replied or asked how much

Write a proposal document with figures (Word-like memo plus HTML slides). Show the owner. Do not send. Cite `.cursor/skills/consulting-proposal/SKILL.md` and `.cursor/skills/show-before-send/SKILL.md`. Optional checkout test `https://buy.stripe.com/3cIcN54OO6SnaA4g5u9ws07` lives in that proposal. Say test, not buy now.
