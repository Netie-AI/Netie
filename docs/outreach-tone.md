## Why the old mails failed

They read like a bot that scraped the site. A Malaysian owner sees:

- `Hi,` then "Public contact from your homepage JSON and contact vCard"
- Subject: "Your services page still says coming soon"
- A litterbox link and two Stripe buttons

That is not a person asking for work. That is a crawler pitching shame.

## What the research says (2026)

1. First touch 50-125 words, often under 80. One CTA. (Autobound / Instantly 2026; The Outbound Game)
2. Open on one researched fact only this firm has. Cosmetic `{FirstName}` is dead. (Zeliq; Unify, 25M emails)
3. Cut AI tells: hope-this-finds-you, moreover, unlock/streamline, rule-of-three adjectives, fake praise. (Unify, Jul 2026)
4. Malaysia: polite, slightly formal greeting (`Dear`, Encik/Puan if known). Soft ask. Do not push. Say where you found the address in human words. Stop when asked. (Jobstreet MY; NineTen; Zenweb on PDPA)
